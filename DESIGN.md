# Design

A personal garden expressed through broad, simple landscape planes rather than literal botanical decoration. Soft leaf green meets deep forest, earth brown, and berry red; the palette lives at the top of `assets/css/style.css`. Generous serif headlines contrast with a plain, readable sans-serif. Notes are open rows rather than boxed cards, and the layout collapses into a single column on small screens. The art is geometric and decorative; content and navigation remain usable without it.

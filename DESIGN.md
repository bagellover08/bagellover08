# Design

A playful, handmade personal homepage inspired by the independent web. The site sits in a centered frame against a dotted forest-green background. Leaf-green borders, cream content panels, dashed rules, and small window-style labels give it an old-web character without compromising readable text or responsive layout. Maroon marks links and active states; brown anchors borders. Palette variables are at the top of `assets/css/style.css`. System fonts keep the site quick and simple to maintain. The navigation, post list, and Monkeytype information remain usable on small screens and without animation.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Jekyll, as requested; simple Markdown authoring.

## Product Purpose
A personal website and digital garden for exploring notes. Content is starter material, not biographical fact.

## Users
Assumption: visitors browse the owner's notes; the owner edits Markdown locally.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Responsive home, garden, about, and sample notes. Colors should be easy to change. No assumed domain purchase, deployment, or hosting provider.

## Brand Commitments
bagellover08; a playful forest-inspired palette of greens, browns, and maroon/red, with room to explore alternatives.
